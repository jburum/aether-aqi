Type and VoiceOver

“Air Quality Forecast” is a placeholder, not the App Store name. Screens are default size at 390×844 pt. Numbers are sample data. No map: that is v1.1.

Dynamic Type

  • Body wraps. The category name is never cut off with an ellipsis.
  • The index may shrink from 84pt to 44pt. It does not overlap the chip.
  • Hourly chips scroll sideways. Their text stays at least 17pt.
  • At the largest sizes, a day stacks: name, bar, maximum, full category.
  • The alerts row stays at least 44pt tall. The switch does not cover its label.

  • The colored bar is hidden. The category word is always spoken.

Other screens

  • Search field is “Search cities”. Results and “Use my location” are buttons. Search works if location is denied.
  • Threshold switch is “Threshold alerts, off”. Stepper is “Notify at 100, not used while alerts are off.”
  • Permission is asked only after the switch is turned on. These mockups stay off.
  • Small, medium, and large widgets speak the same place, index, and category.
