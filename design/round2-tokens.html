Contrast

Every AQI from 0 to 500, in steps of 0.25, keeps primary text at 4.5:1 or better. The low point is 4.63:1, black on #BC5A2A, at AQI 174.5. Ink flips from #000000 to #FFFFFF at AQI 174.75, white on #BA5829 at 4.65:1. The sky gradient above and below a color uses the same ink, and it only deepens when both ends still clear 4.5:1. The top of that gradient keeps its chroma, so good air does not wash out. Secondary ink is that same black or white, mixed toward the background until it still clears 4.5:1.

Hues

Good air is a deep sky blue, around 232°, not a pastel wash. The path crosses the green hues as a stone haze, chroma under 0.03, so it never paints the EPA green chip. Cadmium is in by 150 (#D38026), ember by 200 (#771807), oxblood at 300 (#4B0A0A), and hazardous is near-black red (#1C0304). Hue 250–345° is unused, including gradients and the haze. Reduce Motion steps these stops by category instead of following every AQI.
