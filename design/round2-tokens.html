Contrast

Every AQI from 0 to 500, in steps of 0.25, keeps primary text at 4.5:1 or better. The low point is 4.60:1, white on #C05527, at AQI 185. Ink flips from #000000 to #FFFFFF there. The sky gradient above and below that color uses the same ink, and it only deepens when both ends still clear 4.5:1. Secondary ink is that same black or white, mixed toward the background until it still clears 4.5:1.

Hues

Good air is blue-white, around 236°. The path crosses the green hues as a gray haze, chroma under 0.03, so it never paints the EPA green chip. Amber starts near 100, ember by 200 (#AB4422), oxblood at 300 (#591514), and hazardous is near-black red (#1F0505). Hue 250–345° is unused, including gradients and the haze. Reduce Motion steps these stops by category instead of following every AQI.
