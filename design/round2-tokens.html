Contrast

Every AQI from 0 to 500, in steps of 0.25, keeps primary text at 4.5:1 or better. The low point is 4.62:1, white on #BE5626, at AQI 173.5. Ink flips from #000000 to #FFFFFF at AQI 173.5, after black on #BF5827 at 4.64:1. The sky gradient above and below a color uses the same ink, and it only deepens when both ends still clear 4.5:1. The top of that gradient keeps its chroma, so good air does not wash out. Secondary ink is that same black or white, mixed toward the background until it still clears 4.5:1.

Hues

Good air is a deep green, around 154°, and it is still green at 50 (#51A957). Moderate is yellow by 100 (#C4AB11), unhealthy-for-sensitive-groups is orange by 150 (#D77D20), ember is in by 200 (#761406), oxblood at 300 (#4A070A), and hazardous is near-black red (#1C0304). Hue only falls, from green through yellow and red, so 250–345° stays unused, including gradients. Chroma stays above 0.09 through AQI 200, so the middle of the ramp does not go gray. Reduce Motion steps these stops by category instead of following every AQI.
