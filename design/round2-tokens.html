Contrast

Every AQI from 0 to 500, in steps of 0.25, keeps primary text at 4.5:1 or better. The low point is 4.59:1, black on #C45229, at AQI 189. Ink flips from #000000 to #FFFFFF there. Secondary ink is that same black or white, mixed toward the background until it still clears 4.5:1.

Hues

The path runs 170° green-teal, through yellow and orange, to 24° oxblood. Hazardous #4C1314 is in the #4A0E0E range. Hue 250–345° is unused, including gradients and the haze. Reduce Motion steps these stops by category instead of following every AQI.
