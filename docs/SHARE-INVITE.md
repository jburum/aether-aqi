# Sharing Air Quality Watchlist

```
🌬️ Air Quality Watchlist
Track US AQI for the places you care about.

Add to Home Screen (iPhone):
1. Open the link in Safari
2. Tap Share ⎋ (square with ↑)
3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”
4. Tap Add

```
