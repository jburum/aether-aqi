Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

This policy covers the AirHappy iPhone app and the airhappy.app website. Both are published by BBCVC LLC (“we”, “us”). AirHappy needs to know which places you care about so it can forecast the air there. Beyond that, we keep as little as possible.

The short version

  • No ads, no advertising or analytics SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell or rent your data, and we don’t share it for advertising.
  • Your saved places and alert choices are kept on your phone unless a feature needs our server.
  • Location is optional, used only while the app is open, and only when you ask for it.
  • Apple handles payment. We never see your card or bank details.

What stays on your phone

Your saved places (name and map coordinates), their order, and which alerts you turned on are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What our server receives today

The current AirHappy iPhone app uses sample forecasts and keeps your places on your phone. It does not send your places, purchases, or alert choices to our server. Today our server receives:

  • Website forecasts and place search. When you use the website, it sends a place’s coordinates (or the name you search for) through our server to the data sources listed below. Our server passes these on and does not store them.
  • Sign-in on the website, if you use it. Sign-in goes through the Grok sign-in service at auth.grok.me, which acts as the login broker. We receive the name, email address, and profile picture it shares, plus the sign-in tokens it issues, and store them in our database with your account. Each session we create also stores the IP address and browser details (user agent) of the device you signed in from.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records technical details of each request (IP address, time, the page or data requested, browser or app version) to keep the service running and secure. These logs are not used to build a profile of you.

Planned: when the app connects to our server

The features below are built on our server but not yet used by the app. When the app starts using them, we will update this policy and tell you in the app first.

  • Saved places and forecasts. To check alerts while the app is closed, we would store each saved place’s name, coordinates, alert setting, and the forecasts made for it, linked to your account.
  • Pro subscription status. When you subscribe, Apple gives the app a signed record of the purchase. We would keep which plan you chose (monthly or yearly), when it renews or ends, and whether it is active, so Pro works across devices. We don’t receive your name, email, or payment details from Apple.
  • Notifications. If you turn on alerts and allow notifications, we would keep the push token Apple gives your device so we can deliver alerts, and a record of which alerts were sent so you don’t get the same one twice.

Location

You can always add a place by searching or picking it from a list. If you choose to use your current location, AirHappy asks for permission “while using the app” only. It reads your position once, uses it to name the spot and fetch its forecast, and does not keep a history of where you have been. You can turn location off any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Who else is involved

We use a few service providers to run AirHappy. They process data only to provide their service to us.

  • Apple: App Store purchases, subscriptions, refunds, and push notifications.
  • Grok sign-in (auth.grok.me): the login broker for website sign-in. It handles your login and shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us.
  • Vercel (hosting) and Neon (database): run our server and store the data described above, in the United States.
  • Public air and weather data sources: NOAA, the U.S. EPA AirNow program, and Open-Meteo. Our server sends them the coordinates of a place to get its forecast or latest reading. They don’t receive your name, email, or account ID.
  • Place search on the website: place names you type are sent to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service. If you use “current location” on the website, your coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud to look up the nearest town name, and to Open-Meteo for the air reading.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the service. If BBCVC LLC or AirHappy is sold or merged, this policy continues to apply to data collected under it.

The website

The airhappy.app website keeps your watchlist in your browser’s local storage, not on our server. Clearing your browser data removes it. The website uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Account details (name, email, profile picture, sign-in tokens): until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions, including their IP address and user agent: removed when you sign out. A session you don’t sign out of expires after about a week without use; expired session records can stay in our database until we clean them up or you ask us to delete them.
  • Server logs: kept briefly by our hosting provider, then deleted.
  • Planned features: saved places and their forecasts until you delete the place or your account; subscription status while you have an account; push tokens until you delete your account or ask us to remove them. The app can’t yet remove a token on its own when you turn alerts off, so ask us if you want it gone.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Email support@airhappy.app from any address, and tell us what you’d like. We’ll confirm the request and complete it within 30 days. Deleting your data does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to a data protection authority. We honor these requests wherever you live.

Children

AirHappy is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access limited to the people who run AirHappy. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold small on purpose.

Not medical advice

AirHappy forecasts air quality. It doesn’t give medical advice, and we don’t ask for or keep health information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data we already hold, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
